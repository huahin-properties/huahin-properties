Hua Hin Condos for Sale & Rent

A condominium is the only property type in Thailand a foreign buyer can own outright in their own name, subject to the building's 49% foreign quota. For overseas buyers who want a low-maintenance holiday base with clean, straightforward title, it is almost always the right starting point in Hua Hin.

Three broad location groups define the market. Beachfront on Hua Hin Beach and Khao Takiab Beach carries the highest price per square metre but holds value best and rents throughout the year. Town-centre condos put the night market, the historic railway station and Bangkok Hospital within walking distance, which matters enormously for long-stay retirees. Condos from Soi 70 outwards offer the easiest entry price and the newest facilities, though a car becomes necessary.

Before you commit, request four documents from the juristic person: a debt-free certificate for the unit, written confirmation of whether the unit sits in the Thai or foreign quota, the current common fee rate per square metre, and the sinking fund amount payable on transfer. Foreign buyers must additionally transfer the purchase funds from abroad in foreign currency and obtain a Foreign Exchange Transaction form from the receiving bank — without it, registration of foreign ownership will not proceed.

Details buyers routinely overlook: west-facing units become uncomfortably hot through the afternoon and cost visibly more to cool; ground-floor units beside the pool are noisy through high season; and buildings with many units on daily rental see constant stranger traffic. Ask the juristic person directly whether short-term letting is permitted in the building.

Frequently asked questions

Can foreigners buy a condo in Hua Hin?

Yes — 100% freehold in your own name, provided the unit falls within the building's 49% foreign quota and the purchase funds are remitted from overseas in foreign currency with a Foreign Exchange Transaction form issued by the Thai bank.

What are Hua Hin condo common fees?

Typically THB 35–70 per square metre per month. A 35 sqm unit therefore costs roughly THB 1,200–2,450 monthly. Buildings with extensive facilities sit at the higher end.

What do Hua Hin condos cost?

Studios from Soi 70 outwards start around THB 1.2–2 million. One-bedroom units near town run THB 2.5–4.5 million. Sea-view beachfront units start from THB 5 million.
