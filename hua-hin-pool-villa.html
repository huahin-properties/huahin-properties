Hua Hin Pool Villas for Sale & Rent

A private pool is the single feature that most changes both lifestyle and rental economics in Hua Hin. Villas with their own pool command roughly double the nightly rate of comparable homes without one, and they remain the most liquid resale category in the market — buyers arriving from Bangkok, Scandinavia and Germany almost always shortlist pool villas first.

Understanding the zones matters more than the listing photos. Khao Takiab sits closest to the beach and restaurants, carries the highest price per square wah, and rents most reliably year-round. Soi 70 to Soi 112 is where most new villa projects have been built in the last three years — modern layouts, good value, but you will need a car. Thap Tai and Hin Lek Fai give you noticeably more land and a bigger pool for the same budget, in exchange for a 15-minute drive into town. Pranburi, 25 minutes south, is where the same money buys a substantially larger plot.

Budget honestly for running costs before you buy. Professional pool maintenance runs THB 2,500–4,500 per month including chemicals; pool pump electricity adds THB 800–1,500; garden care is THB 2,000–3,500; and estate common fees in Hua Hin typically fall between THB 30–60 per square wah per month. A 4x8m pool is ample for a family and cheaper to run than a 5x10m, which suits villas built primarily for group rentals.

Two checks that protect resale value: confirm the title is a Chanote (Nor Sor 4 Jor) with a proper survey rather than a lesser deed, and confirm the access road is either public or formally registered as a servitude on the title. Verbal assurances from a previous owner about the road are worth nothing when you come to sell.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a Hua Hin pool villa cost?

Two-bedroom villas in Thap Tai estates start around THB 4.5–6 million. Three-bedroom villas on 80–120 square wah typically sit at THB 7–12 million. Sea-view and luxury hillside villas start from THB 15 million.

Can foreigners own a pool villa in Hua Hin?

A foreigner can own the building outright but not the land in a personal name. The common lawful routes are a registered 30-year lease on the land or ownership through a properly structured Thai company. Always take independent legal advice before signing.

What rental yield can I expect?

Well-managed villas close to the beach typically produce net yields of 4–7% per year, driven mainly by high-season occupancy between November and February.
